# Architecture: Reinforcement

Part of the architecture notes indexed in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Keep each topic in its bullet here; AGENTS.md only lists the topics.

- **Bar marks and bending schedule** — `cc_core::bar_marks`: `Project::bar_marking` (`BarMarking`: `prefix`, `start`, `length_tolerance`, `stock_length`, the frozen marks table `frozen: Vec<FrozenMark>` of `mark`, `diameter`, `grade` and a compact canonical `MarkShape`; tracked as settings, `EditCtx::bar_marking_mut`, serde default, `repair` bounds it, `MAX_FROZEN`, `MAX_MARK_LEGS`). Sets and shaped bars have a `partition` and a `bar_mark` (`Element::rebar_partition`/`rebar_bar_mark`), bar types a `grade` (the metric template's: CA-60 for Ø 5,0, CA-50 from Ø 6,3 up, `TemplateSpec::rebars`, `TypeData::graded_rebar`; the Ø 6,3 type, and the imperial #7, is `TemplateSpec::extra_rebar`, added last after the concrete wall types so the other ids stay; #437) and an optional `mass_per_meter` (`TypeData::rebar`, `RebarGeom::grade`/`mass_per_meter()`, which weights use). `cc_geom::bar_shape::summary` reads a bar's `ShapeSummary` from its center line (points in a line one leg, equal chords turning evenly one arc leg with its radius, a ring one leg of a whole turn; a `true_length` longer than the line, as a tie's hooks, spread over the legs), and `cc_geom::bar_marks::resolve` (pure, over `ModelGeometry::rebar`) gives every bar a `Mark`: a set's `bar_mark` first, then the first frozen entry its shape matches (diameter, grade, `ShapeSummary::matches` within the tolerance and `ANGLE_TOLERANCE_DEG`), then shapes numbered after the highest frozen number in the stable order partition, diameter descending, length descending, `canonical` shape; `BarMarks` sorts marks naturally, with exact counts, lengths and weights per mark (`of_bar`, `of_set`). `cc_views::bending`: `ScheduleDef::bending` (`BendingOptions`: `partitions`, `group_by_partition`, `waste_percent`; serde default, repaired) turns a reinforcement schedule into a row per mark (`bending` gives the exact rows and `DiameterTotal`s, the totals adding the rows, which add their bars; `table` formats them), with `Table::summary` under it (`caption`), group `Row::heading`s and a `BarSketch` per row (`sketch_of`: the shape's legs laid flat in order, short legs drawn longer; `draw_sketch` fits it in the cell, `schedule::drawing` makes those rows `SKETCH_ROW` tall) whose CSV and table text is `shape_code` (`straight 500`, `L 30-470`, `stirrup 14x44`, `stirrup 14x44 hook 135° 5 hook 135° 5`, `arc R=220 L=345`; `format::bar_length` writes centimeters). Bar tags: `ElementKind::BarTag` (`cc_core::bar_tag`, category `BarTag`, `TypeData::BarTag { text_size }` made with the first tag) is view-owned (plans, elevations, sections) and references a set (`set`, removed with it by `remove_element` and repair), with `position`, `leader`, `rotation_deg`, `multiplier` and `parts`; `cc_views::bar_tags` derives the text (`2x 25 N1 Ø12.5 c/12 C=500`) and draws the tagged set's bars in that view through `rebar_view::draw_set`, within the view's range (none when the view shows all its reinforcement, which drew them). `cc_engine::commands::bar_marks`: `bar_marks_list`, `bar_marks_update` (freeze, `drop_unused`), `bar_marks_renumber` (`prefix`, `start`, `keep_overrides`), `bar_marking_set`, `bar_length_check`, `bending_schedule_create`, `bar_tag_create`; the reinforcement commands take `partition`, `element_get` gives a set's `marks` and a tag's `text`, `schedule_table` the `summary`. IFC export writes `Partition`, `BarMark`, `Mark` and `Grade` in `REBAR_PSET` (`ConstructCAD_Rebar`), and import reads the first two and the grade back; a set laid out in a host also writes `Host` and `Layout`, and comes back as a set of the imported host (#368, see the cc_ifc notes in `interop.md`). The app shows headings and the summary in the schedule view; tags are placed by their command.
- **Reinforcement in plans, elevations and sections** — `View::rebar` (serde default, not written when false; `ViewKind::takes_rebar`: floor plans, elevations, sections; `View::shows_rebar` also reads `ViewKind::ThreeD { rebar }`). `cc_views::rebar_view`: `BarRange::of` gives the view's frame with its cut plane and depth (a floor plan looks down from its cut height to the level below, none under the lowest; a section from its line to its depth, within its line's ends, as `projected::view_frame`; an elevation unlimited); `draw_set` clips each bar to it (`Frame::planes`, `clip_segment`), draws a filled dot (`dot`, `DOT_FILL`) as wide as the bar (`dot_radius`: at least `MIN_DOT` paper mm) where a bar crosses the cut plane at least `CUT_STEEPNESS` steeply (cos 0.5; a bar crossing it more obliquely is a line only), the parts within the range as `LineStyle::Thin` polylines tagged with the set, and a leg of an open bar seen end-on within the range (`END_ON`) as a dot, so a starter bar with a hook keeps its dot (closed loops are drawn by their legs, corners not dotted); nothing in front of the cut. A set whose bounding box lies outside a plane of the range is skipped (`BarRange::misses`), and the planes are computed once per view. A ceiling plan has a range without limits seen from above, so its bar tags draw their sets whole, as before. `draw_all` draws every set of a view with `rebar` on, in `generate` after the plan's fills (before its marks and tags) and after a section's or elevation's joins. Bar tags draw their sets through `draw_set` (fixing tags that drew the whole set, beyond the depth). `view_set` `rebar` sets it (in a 3D view, the kind's `rebar`), `view_get` gives `rebar`; the app has a *Show Reinforcement* row (`prop-show-rebar`) in the graphics section of those views' properties (`engine_edit::view`).
- **Bar marks and bending schedule** — `cc_core::bar_marks`: `Project::bar_marking` (`BarMarking`: `prefix`, `start`, `length_tolerance`, `stock_length`, the frozen marks table `frozen: Vec<FrozenMark>` of `mark`, `diameter`, `grade` and a compact canonical `MarkShape`; tracked as settings, `EditCtx::bar_marking_mut`, serde default, `repair` bounds it, `MAX_FROZEN`, `MAX_MARK_LEGS`). Sets and shaped bars have a `partition` and a `bar_mark` (`Element::rebar_partition`/`rebar_bar_mark`), bar types a `grade` (the metric template's: CA-60 for Ø 5,0, CA-50 from Ø 6,3 up, `TemplateSpec::rebars`, `TypeData::graded_rebar`; a `true_length` longer than the line spread over the legs; an open bar with `Bar::end_hooks`, a tie, gets a hook leg at each end, see below), and `cc_geom::bar_marks::resolve` (pure, over `ModelGeometry::rebar`) gives every bar a `Mark`: a set's `bar_mark` first, then the first frozen entry its shape matches (diameter, grade, `ShapeSummary::matches` within the tolerance and `ANGLE_TOLERANCE_DEG`), then shapes numbered after the highest frozen number in the stable order partition, diameter descending, length descending, `canonical` shape; `BarMarks` sorts marks naturally, with exact counts, lengths and weights per mark (`of_bar`, `of_set`). `cc_views::bending`: `ScheduleDef::bending` (`BendingOptions`: `partitions`, `group_by_partition`, `waste_percent`; serde default, repaired) turns a reinforcement schedule into a row per mark (`bending` gives the exact rows and `DiameterTotal`s, the totals adding the rows, which add their bars; `table` formats them), with `Table::summary` under it (`caption`), group `Row::heading`s and a `BarSketch` per row (`sketch_of`: the shape's legs laid flat in order, short legs drawn longer; `draw_sketch` fits it in the cell, `schedule::drawing` makes those rows `SKETCH_ROW` tall) whose CSV and table text is `shape_code` (`straight 500`, `L 30-470`, `stirrup 14x44`, `stirrup 14x44 hook 135° 5 hook 135° 5`, `tie 20 hook 135° 5 hook 180° 5`, `arc R=220 L=345`; `format::bar_length` writes centimeters). Bar tags: `ElementKind::BarTag` (`cc_core::bar_tag`, category `BarTag`, `TypeData::BarTag { text_size }` made with the first tag) is view-owned (plans, elevations, sections) and references a set (`set`, removed with it by `remove_element` and repair), with `position`, `leader`, `rotation_deg`, `multiplier` and `parts`; `cc_views::bar_tags` derives the text (`2x 25 N1 Ø12.5 c/12 C=500`) and draws the tagged set's bars as thin lines in that view (no other view draws bars in 2D). `cc_engine::commands::bar_marks`: `bar_marks_list`, `bar_marks_update` (freeze, `drop_unused`), `bar_marks_renumber` (`prefix`, `start`, `keep_overrides`), `bar_marking_set`, `bar_length_check`, `bending_schedule_create`, `bar_tag_create`; the reinforcement commands take `partition`, `element_get` gives a set's `marks` and a tag's `text`, `schedule_table` the `summary`. IFC export writes `Partition`, `BarMark`, `Mark` and `Grade` in `REBAR_PSET` (`ConstructCAD_Rebar`), and import reads the first two and the grade back; a set laid out in a host also writes `Host` and `Layout`, and comes back as a set of the imported host (#368, see the cc_ifc notes in `interop.md`). The app shows headings and the summary in the schedule view; tags are placed by their command.
- **Stirrup hooks** — `RebarLayout::Stirrups { spacing, hooks: Option<StirrupHooks> }` (`cc_core::StirrupHooks { angle, tail }`: degrees in `StirrupHooks::ANGLES`, 90 to 180, and the straight end in bar diameters up to `MAX_TAIL`, at least `MIN_TAIL_LENGTH` 5 cm; `Default` 135° and 10 diameters; serde default `None`, not written when `None`, so older files keep their stirrups without hooks and their lengths; invalid hooks make the set invalid like a wall tie's angles). `cc_geom::rebar::LoopHooks` (`of`, the bend's center-line radius `PIN_RADIUS` 2 diameters as a tie's) is set on every stirrup `Bar` (`Bar::hooks`: beams, strip beams, columns along the outline, round, sectors, polygons and `overlapping` rectangles); the loop is not drawn with them, `Bar::length` adds `2 × (angle × radius + tail)` to the loop (sides to the sharp corners, or `true_length`), and a column's outline starts at its sharpest convex corner (`from_convex_corner`), where the hooks are. `bar_shape::summary` reads a hooked loop from that corner as an open shape `[hook, loop legs…, hook]` (hook legs `hook: true`, their length the straight end, bent by the hook's angle the way the loop turns; the bends' arcs are in the bar's length, not in the legs), and `ShapeSummary::hooked_loop` gives back the closed loop (its closing turn what the others leave of a whole turn) and the two hooks; `bending::shape_code` writes `stirrup 14x44 hook 135° 5 hook 135° 5` and `sketch_of` draws the hooks at the closing corner without labels. `rebar_create`, `rebar_typical` and `strip_rebar_create` `beam` take `hook` (0 for none) and `tail` (`commands::rebar::stirrup_hooks`), new sets getting 135° and 10 diameters; `element_set` edits `layout.Stirrups.hooks` (null for none). IFC export writes the loop as the bar's directrix and the hooked length in `BarLength`, as for ties. The app: *Stirrup Hooks* (none, 90°, 135°) and *Hook Tail* (diameters) rows in a stirrup set's *Reinforcement* section (`properties::stirrup_hook_rows`).
- **Tie hooks** — column cross-ties (`RebarLayout::Ties`) and wall ties (`RebarLayout::WallTies`) are drawn as their straight part between the bars they hold, with `Bar::end_hooks` (`cc_geom::rebar::EndHooks { start, end, opposite }`, two `LoopHooks` and whether the end hook bends to the other side; `EndHooks::of_tie`: bend around `PIN_RADIUS`, straight end of 5 diameters, at least 5 cm) and a `true_length` adding the two hooks (`EndHooks::length`). Column ties are 135° at their start and 180° at their end, both to one side (a C); wall ties take `hooks` [exterior, interior] and bend to opposite sides (an S). `bar_shape::summary` reads such a bar as `[hook, its legs…, hook]` (`with_end_hooks`: hook legs `hook: true`, their length the straight end; the first bent by the start hook's angle, the bar's last leg by the end hook's, the same way or, for an S, the other), and `ShapeSummary::hooked_ends` gives back the bar and the two hooks; `hooked_loop` only reads hook legs as closing a loop when the legs between them close on themselves (`closes`). `bending::shape_code` writes `tie 20 hook 135° 5 hook 180° 5` and `sketch_of` draws the hooks bent around a small pin (`TIE_PIN`), so a 180° hook stands off the bar, without labels. Lengths and weights are as before; bar marks tell C and S ties, and different hooks, apart (#438).
- **Anchorage of beam bars** (#346) — `Rebar::anchorage: Option<cc_core::Anchorage { length, hook: AnchorHook }>`: the length in bar diameters (`DEFAULT_LENGTH` 40, `MIN_LENGTH` 10 to `MAX_LENGTH` 100), `AnchorHook` none, down, up or auto (`direction` by `BarFace`: auto bends top bars down and bottom bars up; side bars never bend), `MIN_HOOK` 8 diameters; serde default none, not written when none (no format change); `Anchorage::fits` the `Longitudinal` and `Sides` layouts; repair resets one out of range, or on a set that is not a beam's longitudinal or side bars, to none and keeps the set, so `element_set` refuses it. `cc_geom::rebar`: `Supports::of` (built only when a set is anchored) keeps each support's `ColumnInput` (`build::support_input`, shared with `build_beams`: a structural column's convex hull, a pile cap that is not a ring) and the beam ends framed into it; `Supports::anchors` gives a set an `EndAnchor` at each `BeamEnd::Column` end of its beam, `in_line` when another beam framed into that support leaves it the opposite way (within `IN_LINE`) at a common depth. `anchor_end` finds where the bar's line (`polygon::clip_line`) or circle (`support_span`: the crossings of the circle with the outline's sides, placed along the axis from the end face, half a turn either way) runs through the support behind the end face (within `SUPPORT_GAP`), so a turned or round support is met where the bar meets it: the bar runs on past the near face up to the far face less the cover, at most the anchorage length; where that is short a top or bottom bar ends in a vertical 90° leg making up the rest (at least `MIN_HOOK`, at most the support's height less the cover, else none); in line it runs half the support and half a lap (`lap_of`: the splice's, else 40 diameters) past the near face, still at most to the far face less the cover, without a hook, so two beams' bars lap inside the support; `None` (the bar stops the cover short of the end as before) where the line misses the support, the bar lies above or below it, or the cover leaves no room. `BeamRun` is the developed bar (legs and run; corners sharp, the legs measured to the run's line as a wall bar's `BarEnd` legs are, no bend deduction), cut by `Splice::pieces` after the anchorage is added (`piece`: legs, and the run in chords along an arc with the developed `true_length`). An end framed into a support takes the anchorage and an end joined to another beam `lap_at_joins`, never both; stirrups are unchanged. `bar_shape` reads a hooked bar as an `L` or `U` (`bent` along an arc). `cc_engine::commands::rebar`: `rebar_create` and `rebar_typical` (the beams' top and bottom sets) take `anchor` (`AnchorArg`: `true` or diameters) and `anchor_hook`, refused on stirrups and columns; `element_set` edits `anchorage` (null for none). The app: *Anchor into Supports* and *Anchorage Hook* rows of a beam's longitudinal and side sets (`properties::anchorage_rows`; no hook row for side bars; the length is set by commands). Tests: `cc_engine/tests/beam_anchorage.rs`.
- **Wall reinforcement** — `cc_engine::commands::wall_rebar`: `wall_rebar_create` (`walls`, or the structural walls of a level or the project; `faces` both/exterior/interior, `cover`/`cover_exterior`/`cover_interior`, `horizontal { type, spacing | zones }`, `vertical { type, spacing, offset, from, to, bottom, top }`, `ties { type, every_horizontal, every_vertical, hooks }` (the two hook angles, kept in `RebarLayout::WallTies::hooks`), `outer` horizontal/vertical, `max_length`, `lap`, `lap_at_joins`; with no family given, horizontal and vertical bars every 15 cm; curtain walls refused; one `Rebar` set per family and face, one undo step, `{created, walls, skipped, not_structural}`, with a `warning` when walls chosen by level or project are all left out for not being structural; a wall is structural by default when its type is, as the templates' concrete wall types are); `rebar_create` stays for beams and columns. `rebar_copy` (`commands::rebar`: `from`, `to`, `sets`, `replace`) copies a host's sets to hosts of the same kind and section (`host_kind`: column, beam, wall, strip; `same_section`: the same type, or column and beam types whose placed outlines match), refusing floors and slab foundations, one undo step, `{created, replaced}`. `Rebar::lap_at_joins` (also `strip_rebar_create` and `rebar_create` for beams) runs the bars along a host half a lap past each end where another host carries on in the same line (`cc_geom::rebar::carries_on` over the ends of the hosts' axes, `run_past`), so the rings of a cylinder, a ring footing or a ring beam lap at the joints; `Rebar::overlapping` makes a column's stirrups one closed rectangle around each of the rectangles of a section of square corners (`cc_geom::faces::rectangles`), and `Rebar::lifts` cuts a column's vertical bars at given heights (`Rebar::lift_pieces`); all with serde defaults, checked by `Rebar::is_valid`. The sets are ordinary `Rebar` elements hosted by the wall (removed with it, in rebar schedules, 3D rebar views and IFC as `IfcReinforcingBar`). The app: a structural wall's *Reinforcement* section in the properties palette (`properties::reinforcement`, shared with beams and columns), whose typical button runs the command. `Session::geom` is the cached geometry: after a command that does not return an element, tests read `Session::geometry`.
- **Slab reinforcement** — floors and foundation slabs host `Rebar` sets with the layouts `SlabStraight { top, angle_deg, spacing, inner, hook }`, `SlabRing { top, spacing, center, inner }`, `SlabRadial { top, count, center, start, intermediate, inner }` and `SlabTrim { top, count, spacing, anchorage, diagonals }` (`RebarLayout::in_slab`, `slab_top`; limits in `cc_core::slab_bars`; repair keeps them in floors and foundation slabs). `cc_geom::slab_rebar` lays them out: `Slab::of` reads the outline, openings (a floor's own and its shafts' cuts), the circles of its sketch and the bottom and top of its structure (`core_range`); `Region` is the slab less the cover (the boundary moved in, openings grown, with `polygon::offset`; empty when a side flips), whose `stretches` of a line are found by even-odd crossings and, on a side that is a chord of a sketched circle, moved onto the circle itself, so bars end on true arcs; `arcs` clips a circle. Mesh lines run either side of the one through the middle of the slab's box, each stretch a bar cut by `Rebar::splice`, with a vertical leg (`hook`) only at ends on the slab's own edge; rings are closed, or with a splice pieces lapping around; trimming bars run along the sides of each opening's box and across its corners, in a region a hair wider so the line the cover draws is inside. An `inner` set lies a bar of the layer's other sets further in. `cc_engine::commands::slab_rebar`: `slab_rebar_create` (`slabs`, or the structural floors, `Floor::structural`, and the foundation slabs of a level or the project; `layout` mesh/radial, `layers`, `type`/`cross_type`, `spacing`/`cross_spacing`, `angle`, `hook`, `center`, `radial_count`, `start`, `intermediate`, `cover`, `trim` (values or `false`; made only for slabs with openings), `max_length`, `lap`; one undo step, `{created, slabs}`); `element_get` of any rebar set lists `derived.bars` (`{length, count}` to the millimeter, longest first). The app: a floor's *Structural* checkbox, and the *Reinforcement* section of a structural floor's or a foundation slab's properties.
- **Strip footings with a beam, and curved rafts** — a strip footing type may be an inverted T: `TypeData::Foundation::haunch` (`cc_core::StripHaunch { edge_thickness, beam_width, beam_height }`: the footing slab is the type's `thickness` deep at the faces of the beam and `edge_thickness` at its edges, the beam rises `beam_height` above it; `fits`, `outline`, `area`; `TypeData::strip_haunch` and `foundation_depth`, the top of the foundation being the top of the beam; repair drops a haunch that makes no section). Every strip's `FoundationGeom::strip` keeps its `BeamGeom` (frame, section, ends after joins), and `FoundationGeom::body()` is that of a haunched one, swept with `BeamGeom::add_to` (straight or arc) with its exact `volume`; plans add the beam's two faces (`BeamGeom::line`), legends draw the T; IFC export writes one whose ends are square and level as its section swept exactly (`Exporter::strip_sweep`: an arc revolved about the arc's vertical axis, `IfcRevolvedAreaSolid`; a straight one extruded level along it), which import reads back as a native strip of the same type (#435), and any other (sloped, or ends cut at an angle by joins) as a faceted body. A strip hosts `Rebar` sets (`RebarLayout::fits_strip`): its own `StripTransverse { top, spacing }` and `StripLongitudinal { top, spacing }` (`cc_geom::strip_rebar`: `StripSection::top(v)` is the height of the footing's top across it; a top bar across goes down to the bottom layer at each edge, up the slopes and flat between the beam's faces; bars along follow the slopes at their own radius on an arc, top ones leaving the beam's width, and lie inside the bars across), and a beam's `Stirrups`, `Sides` and top/bottom `Longitudinal`, laid out by `beam_bars` in a beam as wide as the beam part (`StripSection::beam_half`) and as deep as the whole section. `cc_engine::commands::strip_rebar`: `strip_rebar_create` (`strips` or those of a level, `layers`, `type`/`longitudinal_type`, `transverse_spacing`, `longitudinal_spacing` (0 for none), `beam { type, top, bottom, sides, stirrup_type, stirrups }` or `false`: a haunched strip gets stirrups and two top bars by default, a plain one only when asked; `cover`, `max_length`, `lap`; `{created, strips}`). A foundation slab keeps the curves it was drawn with (`Foundation::sketch`, `Foundation::new`, `slab_area`; `foundation_slab_create` `curves`, `sketch_get`/`sketch_set`), so `FoundationGeom::drawn_area` and its volume are exact and `slab_rebar` clips bars to its circles. The app: the *Beam on the footing* section of a strip type's Type Properties (`type_dialog::strip_haunch_rows`) and the *Reinforcement* section of a strip's properties.
- **Pile caps and piles** (#343) — pile caps (`Project::pile_cap`) host `RebarLayout::CapBottom { count, spacing, diagonals, bend }`, `CapTop { spacing, bend }`, `CapSides { spacing, hooks }` and `Stirrups`; piles host `PileLongitudinal { count, spacing, length, projection }`, `PileSpiral { pitch, length }` and `PileTies { spacing, length, hooks }` (`RebarLayout::in_cap`, `fits_cap`, `in_pile`; limits in `cc_core::pile_bars`: `MAX_BAND`, `MAX_AROUND`, `MAX_BEND`, `MAX_LENGTH` the longest pile, `MAX_PITCH`; serde defaults on the optional fields, no format change, as for the strip and slab variants). `Project::rebar_host_fits` (repair and IFC import) keeps cap layouts in pads that are pile caps, pile layouts in piles, and neither in beams or columns; an edit that would leave one elsewhere (a cap's type turned into a plain pad's while it has sets) is refused by the edit check. Every family is laid out by its role from the cap's geometry when the model is built (`cc_geom::cap_rebar`, `CapInput` from `FoundationGeom::cap`, the type's `PileLayout`, embedment and pile size), so a type's layout change re-lays the sets: bottom bars are a band of `count` bars between each two piles of a triangle (its three sides) or a square (its four sides, and with `diagonals` its two diagonals), each as wide as a pile (`spread` over ±(size − d)/2) on the line through the two piles' axes; `count` bars along the line of a pair or a `Line` (`is_beam_like`) spread across the cap's width less the inset; a mesh `spacing` apart both ways (`stations`, first direction along `u`) in a single-pile, round or ring cap (`Region`: the outline moved in, exact chords of a disk, two pieces either side of a ring's hole; offsets on an edge are moved a hair inside). Bands that cross lie in layers a bar apart; the lowest bar lies on the piles' heads, `max(cover, embedment)` above the cap's bottom; ends turn up (bottom) or down (top) the side faces by `bend`, at most to the other face less the cover; bottom and top bars are spliced (`cut` along the developed bar). Side loops (`CapSides`, closed by `LoopHooks` at a convex corner like stirrups; circles exact through `true_length`; both faces of a ring) sit at most `spacing` apart between the bottom and top layers (`side_heights`); `Stirrups` are laid out only across a beam-like cap, as a beam's, and nothing elsewhere. Nesting: stirrups at the cover, side loops inside them, bottom and top bars inside both. Piles (`cc_geom::pile_rebar`, `PileInput`): bars around the section (round: `count` or `ceil(2πr / spacing)` evenly on the circle; square: `faces::outline_faces` and `face_bars`, corners first) inside the spiral or ties, from `length` below the head (0: the toe plus the cover) to `projection` above it, under a cap at most to the cap's top less the cover; cut in lifts by `Rebar::column_pieces`. A spiral is one bar with `Bar::helix` (`Helix { radius, pitch, turns }`, `turns = height / pitch`, not necessarily whole, no closing turns), drawn in chords (at most `ShapedRebar::MAX_POINTS`) with `true_length` = turns × √((2πr)² + pitch²); a square pile's spiral winds on its inscribed circle. `bar_shape::summary` reads a helix as one coiled leg (`LegSummary::pitch`, sweep = turns × 2π), kept in frozen marks (`MarkLeg::pitch`, serde default, `MAX_MARK_TURNS`), `bending::shape_code` writes `spiral R=15 p=10 L=5546` and `sketch_of` draws a short zigzag. A pile kept by a layout change keeps its sets (piles keep their ids); a pile removed takes its sets along (`remove_element`); a pile the change adds takes copies of the sets of the cap's first reinforced pile (`EditCtx::sync_piles`), in the same undo step. Copies of a cap or pile do not take its sets, as no host's copy does (`rebar_copy` copies them, now also between caps and between piles of one type); a mirror moves them with the host. `cc_engine::commands::pile_rebar`: `pile_cap_rebar_create` (`caps`, or the caps of a `level` or the project without reinforcement; `type`, `bottom`, `top`, `sides`, `stirrups` each values or `false`; bottom `count` 4, `bend` 20 diameters, top 8 mm straight, sides 8 mm, stirrups 5 mm only in beam-like caps; `cover`, `max_length`, `lap`, `partition`; one undo step; `{created, caps, skipped: [{cap, reason}]}`, a cap too small for the cover skipped through `cc_geom::pile::cap_room_problem`) and `pile_rebar_create` (`piles`, `caps`, or the piles of a level or the project without reinforcement; `count` (6 round, 4 square) or `spacing`, `length`, `projection` (40 diameters), `transverse` spiral (round) / ties (square) / none, `transverse_type`, `pitch`, `hook`, `tail`; `{created, piles, skipped}`). IFC export writes the sets with `Host` and `Layout` (bottom, top and pile bars `MAIN`, side loops `EDGE`, stirrups, ties and spirals `LIGATURE`), and import lays them out again in the imported cap or pile. Bar tags write the spacing of top meshes, ties and spirals. The app: the *Reinforcement* section of a pile cap's and a pile's properties (*Typical Reinforcement* runs the commands; `properties::pile_rebar_rows`: *Mesh Spacing*, *Diagonal Bands*, *Bent Ends*, *Reinforced Length*, *Projection into Cap*; hooks of side loops and ties), and *Pile Cap Reinforcement* and *Pile Reinforcement* in the Structure tab's *Reinforcement* group (`UiCommand::PileCapRebar`, `PileRebar`: the selection, else the active level). Tests: `cc_engine/tests/pile_rebar.rs`.
