# Architecture: Structure: piles, profiles, member hosts, connections

Part of the architecture notes indexed in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Keep each topic in its bullet here; AGENTS.md only lists the topics.

- **Piles and pile caps** — `cc_core::pile`: `ElementKind::Pile(Pile)` (category `Pile`, `TypeData::Pile { section: PileSection (Round/Square), kind: PileKind, material }`, a round 40 cm concrete type made with the first pile) stands at `position` (and `rotation_deg`) from `cut_off` (its head) down to `toe`, offsets from its level (the length is derived; commands take `toe` or `length`), with an `embedment` into the cap above and an optional `cap: PileCapRef { cap, index }`. A pile cap is a foundation placed as a `FoundationShape::Pad` (position, rotation) whose type is `FoundationKind::PileCap` with `TypeData::Foundation::pile_cap: Some(PileCap)` (layout: single, pair, triangle, square, `Line { count }`, `Circle { count, radius, ring_width }`; spacing, edge distance, pile type, length, embedment; `thickness` is the cap's, `width`/`length` the outline's size); `PileCap::positions` and `outline` (`CapOutline`: polygon, disk or ring, exact `area`) are in the cap's frame (`u` along its rotation). `EditCtx::finish` → `sync_piles` (before phases and options) keeps every cap's piles in step (`Project::pile_sync`): missing indices added (next mark), extra ones and those of no cap removed, the others put where the layout and the cap's placement say (head at the cap's bottom plus the embedment, its level and type), and cap types' width and length; so a type change adds or removes piles in its undo step, a cap's piles move, turn and change with it and go back when moved alone. They are deleted (`remove_element`, `Project::piles_of`) and copied with the cap (`copy_set`; a pile copied alone is free), follow its phases and design option (`phase_host`); repair drops piles whose cap is gone or no cap, outside the layout or doubled, and fixes values out of the module's limits (`MAX_PILES`, `MIN_/MAX_*`). `cc_geom::pile`: `ModelGeometry::piles` (`PileGeom`: axis, section, `z_range`), round piles and round caps as cylinders of smooth facets, ring caps as annuli, `FoundationGeom::cap` (`CapGeom`: frame, outline, pile positions) and `holes`, exact volumes (`curved_volumes` for piles, `FoundationGeom::drawn_area` for caps); `build_structure` feeds caps (not rings) to the column joins, so beams stop at their outlines (`element_get`: `{"pile_cap": id}`). Plans draw caps as foundations and piles dashed with a center mark; schedules have `CutOff`, `Toe`, `PileKind` and, for foundations, `PileCount`. `cc_engine::commands::piles`: `pile_create`, `pile_type_create`, `pile_cap_type_create`, `pile_cap_create`, `pile_caps_under_columns` (`rotation`: `column`, an angle or `radial` about `center`), `tie_beams_create` (ordinary beams, straight or arcs about `center`); `element_set` refuses a cap's pile and takes a free pile's `length`; `elements_delete` keeps a cap's piles deleted alone (`cap_piles_skipped`). IFC export writes `IfcPile` (with `IfcPileType`; kind as predefined and construction types, `export::pile_ifc`) and `IfcFooting` `PILE_CAP` (true circles), with `PILE_PSET` (`ConstructCAD_Pile`) and `PILE_CAP_PSET` (`ConstructCAD_PileCap`); import restores caps and links their piles by the cap's GlobalId (`Importer::link_piles`), and any `IfcPile` that is a plain vertical extrusion of a circle or a square becomes a free pile; another program's `PILE_CAP` (a rectangle, disk or ring) becomes a native cap, its free piles linked, when they stand where one of its layouts puts them (`Importer::foreign_cap`, #370, see `interop.md`). Caps and piles are reinforced by `pile_cap_rebar_create` and `pile_rebar_create` (#343): their sets are laid out from the cap's layout when the model is built, so they follow a type change, and a pile the sync adds takes copies of the sets of the cap's first reinforced pile (see *Pile caps and piles* in `reinforcement.md`).
- **Structural profiles** — `cc_core::structural_profile`: `ProfileDef` (id, `Uid`, unique `name`, `ProfileMaterial` steel/cold-formed/timber/concrete, `ProfileShape`, optional `CatalogRef`, description) lives in `Project::profiles` (tracked as settings; format 27). A `ProfileShape` is a parametric standard shape (I, asymmetric I, channel, T, angle, cold-formed lipped U/Z/hat, rectangular and round tubes, rectangle, circle, concrete T/L, and `AnnularSector`: the piece of a ring between `r_in` and `r_out` turning `sweep` degrees, symmetric about Y with its outer face up, an even number of chords, its exact `area`, `sector_center`; `Project::type_sector` gives a type's ring as placed) or a `ParamSketch` (parameters and expression `ProfilePoint`s, with openings); `outline()` turns either into a `SectionOutline` (outer loop and holes, arcs split every `ARC_STEP`; bounded by the module's `MAX_*`, errors as `ProfileError` values; repair drops bad shapes), and `area()` the exact area (outlines of 8 and 16 chords per quarter turn, extrapolated), which column and beam volumes (`ModelGeometry::curved_volumes`) and IFC quantities of parametric profiles use. `cc_core::section_props` computes area, inertias, moduli, radii and torsion constant from an outline, and `cc_core::profile_catalog` reads the built-in catalogs from the CSV assets in `crates/cc_core/assets/profiles` (W, HP, CS, CVS, U-LAM, L-LAM, BR, HEA/HEB/IPE, CHS/RHS/SHS, NBR 6355 cold-formed, sawn timber, glulam). `TypeData::Column/Beam` hold `profile: Option<ProfileId>` plus `profile_rotation_deg` and `profile_mirror`; `Project::type_section` gives a type's outline and properties (legacy fixed sections are converted to profiles on load, `convert_sections_to_profiles`, and `type_set` refuses `profile: null`), and `sync_profiles` (run by `EditCtx::finish`) keeps each type's width/height bounding-box cache in step with its profile inside the same transaction, so a profile edit resizes its types in one undo step (loading rewrites the same box, `resync_profile_boxes`); readers that need the shape (round rebar, tags, schedules, legends) ask `Project::section_is_round`/`type_profile_def`, not the legacy fields. `cc_geom::section::detailed` builds the cut outline at a detail level (Fine keeps the arcs, Medium squares their corners, Coarse is the bounding box); beams are swept from it along their axes and frame into the convex hull of the placed column outline; plans and sections hatch it in the type's material, tags show a steel or cold-formed profile's name, and schedules have `Profile` and `LinearMass` (kg/m from the material density; the template's steel is 7850 kg/m³). `element_get` gives `derived.section`; analytical export (format 2) writes each member's `section { profile, area, ix, iy, j, width, height }`; format 3 adds a curved member's `arc { bulge, center, radius, sweep }` (degrees) with the arc's `length`, omitted for straight members, and `analytical_model`/`analytical_export` take `arc_segments` to write arcs as that many straight members. IFC export writes the parametric `IfcIShapeProfileDef`/`UShape`/`TShape`/`LShape`/`CShape`/`RectangleHollow`/`CircleHollow`/`Rectangle`/`Circle` where one matches and `IfcArbitraryClosedProfileDef`/`WithVoids` for the rest (cold Z, asymmetric I, other cold shapes, concrete T/L, sketches, mirrored types), with an `IfcMaterialProfileSet` and `IfcMaterialProfileSetUsage` per type; import makes native profiles and types only when it holds the geometry exactly (reusing a profile whose shape matches within 1e-6 m; cold-formed sections marked by the material profile `Category` `ColdFormed`), else components; sketched profiles that cross themselves or whose openings cross or overlap are refused. `cc_engine::commands::profiles`: `profiles_list`, `profile_get`, `profile_create`, `profile_set`, `profile_duplicate`, `profile_delete` (`replace_with`), `profile_catalog`, `profile_import`, `profile_type_create`; `type_set` takes `profile` by name. The app: `profiles_dialog.rs` (the Profile Manager, *Manage → Profiles*, with *Project* and *Catalog* tabs in `LeftTab` for live preview and one-click import) with `profile_sketch.rs` (*Edit sketch* opens `sketch_mode::Target::Section { profile }` with reference planes and keeps the dialog's `dialog::session` alive through `Dialogs::profiles_resume`), and the *Profile* picker of Type Properties in `type_dialog.rs`. Regional structural materials are material libraries (see *Material libraries* in [core.md](core.md)). 
- **Structure by material** — `cc_core::structural_material`: `StructuralMaterial` (concrete, steel, timber; `code`, `from_code`, `key`) and `Project::structural_material`/`types_of_material` (a column or beam type's material comes from its profile's `ProfileMaterial`, else its material class). `cc_engine::commands::structure_material` holds `structural_type_for` (the first type of a material, else a default from the catalog in one undo step: `W200x26,6`, `MLC 9x32`, concrete `20x40`) and `cc_engine::commands::grid_frame` `grid_frame_create` (columns on every storey of a level range and beams on the upper levels over the grids, `secondary` beams per bay, `MAX_MEMBERS`, one undo step). The app: the Structure tab's *Material* selector (`ribbon::structure_tab`, `Preferences::structure_material`), `Tool::Column/Beam::material` filtering `tools::tool_types`, and `grid_frame_ui.rs` (the Grid Frame dialog, which frames the selected grids, else all).
- **Steel buildings** — `cc_core::steel_building`: `ElementKind::SteelBuilding(SteelBuilding)` (category `SteelBuilding`, a `TypeData::SteelBuilding {}` type created with the first one) is a parametric host with no geometry of its own: `origin`, `rotation_deg`, `span`, `eave_height`, `slope_percent`, `FrameRoof` (gable or mono), `bays` and `bay_spacing`, the `column_type` and `rafter_type`, and optional `purlins` and `girts` (`Secondary`: type and spacing), with bounds and defaults as constants (`repair_member_hosts` resets out-of-range values). `layout` works out the portal frames' columns, rafters, purlins and girts, each member keyed by a `MemberRole` (`RoleKind`), at most `MAX_MEMBERS`; the members (`FrameMember`) are ordinary columns and beams. `EditCtx::finish` → `sync_member_hosts` (`cc_core::member_host`, shared with trusses; right after `sync_profiles`, from `element_changes`/`changed_types`): deleted members, and members whose kind, type or level changed while the building did not, are detached (`SteelBuilding::detached`: that role is not made again); changed or added buildings, and buildings whose types changed, regenerate their members in place keeping ids, and a copy gets its own; `remove_element` takes the members along (`Project::host_members_of`) and `copy_set` leaves them out; `repair_member_hosts`; `SteelBuilding` implements `MemberHost`. `cc_engine::commands::steel_building` holds `steel_building_create` (types default through `structure_material::catalog_type` with `FRAME_DEFAULT`/`SECONDARY_DEFAULT`, one undo step); `element_get` shows `derived.members` and `detached`, and `element_set` patches the parameters and refuses a changed `members`. Plans draw the footprint dashed (`plan::draw_steel_buildings`); the host builds no geometry, and IFC export leaves it out (its members are written as columns and beams). The app's `steel_building_ui.rs`: the dialog (*Structure → Framing → Steel Building*), the palette rows and *Reset Members*.
- **Member hosts and trusses** — `cc_core::member_host`: `MemberHost` (member types, layout, member list and detached roles as `RoleKey`s whose order is the typed roles', `role_fits`, `check_parameters`, `repair_parameters`, `inputs`: the elements whose change, type edit or departing hosted element regenerates the host, and `member_level`), `Project::member_host_of`, `ElementKind::member_host`/`member_host_mut`, and the generic `sync_member_hosts`, `repair_member_hosts` and `host_members_of`. `cc_core::truss`: `ElementKind::Truss(Truss)` (category `Truss`, a `TypeData::Truss {}` type created with the first one) holds the `start`/`end` span line, `base_offset` of the bottom chord's axis, a `TrussShape` (flat, mono, gable) with `rise`, `height`, a `TrussPattern` (Pratt, Howe, Warren, king post, queen post, Fink; `pattern_used` falls back to Pratt where a pattern does not fit), `panels` 2 to 64 (even on a gable), the top chord, bottom chord, web and post types, and `copies` at `spacing` to the left of the span line; `layout(&Sections)` makes one continuous chord beam per straight run (a gable's top chord is two; joins off, so they pass their posts), diagonals between panel points (`BeamVertical::Center`) and post columns (a flat truss has posts at both supports), keyed by `TrussRole`/`TrussRoleKind`, skipping verticals under `MIN_VERTICAL`; `Sections::of` reads the member types' sections: the posts are turned so the side nearest the chords' width lies across the truss and stand where they `seat` on each chord (its face, or inside a channel open toward the web; under a sloped chord, its underside), and where the truss ends on a post the chords run on to its far face and the far post is turned 180°, closing the ends; an older file's per-panel chords are replaced when the truss regenerates; `check` refuses a truss whose layout (all copies, `members_per_copy`) would pass `MAX_MEMBERS`; a mirror maps the copies. `cc_engine::commands::framing` holds `truss_create` (`material` steel or timber, `truss_rows` through `structure_material::catalog_type`/`type_or_row`, the steel rows' channels laid on their sides with `catalog_type_placed`: bottom chord open upward, top chord and diagonals mirrored; one undo step, returns `{id, members}`; `element_get` shows `derived.members`, `detached` and `pattern_used`). Plans draw each copy's span line dashed (`plan::draw_trusses`); IFC export leaves the host out. The app: `Tool::Truss { material }` (in the steel and timber framing groups, `TrussOptions`, `truss_command`), `truss_ui.rs` (options bar, palette rows) and `member_host_ui.rs` (member counts and *Reset Members*, shared with `steel_building_ui.rs`).
- **Bracing** — `cc_core::bracing`: `ElementKind::Bracing(Bracing)` (category `Bracing`, a `TypeData::Bracing {}` type created with the first one) is a member host of the braces of one bay of a frame: the bay line `start`/`end` (two column axes), `base_offset`, the storey `height`, `stories` 1 to 100, a `BracingPattern` (`V`, `Chevron`, `Diagonal`, `X`, `K`: braces from the mid-height of each column to the middle of the bay at the top and bottom of the storey; `per_story`), `flip` and `alternate` (diagonals only) and the `brace_type` (a beam type); `layout` makes the braces as sloped beams (`BeamVertical::Center`, `end_offset`), keyed by `BracingRole { story, index }`. `cc_engine::commands::bracing` holds `bracing_create` (`start`/`end`, or two `columns` whose level, base and common height it takes; `brace_row` through `type_or_row`: steel `SHS 100x100x4`, timber `Peça 6x12`, concrete needs a `type`; one undo step, returns `{id, members}`; `element_get` shows `derived.members`, `detached`, `length`, `total_height`); the older `brace_create` and `braces_between_columns` still make loose beams. Plans draw the bay line dashed (`plan::draw_trusses`); IFC export leaves the host out. The app: `UiCommand::Bracing` in the steel and timber *Structure* groups (`bracing_ui::start`: two selected columns are braced at once, else `Tool::Bracing { material }`, `BracingOptions`, `bracing_command`), and `bracing_ui.rs` (options bar, palette rows).
- **Wall framing** — `cc_core::wall_framing`: `ElementKind::WallFraming(WallFraming)` (category `WallFraming`, a `TypeData::WallFraming {}` type created with the first one) is a member host of one straight wall: the `wall`, a `FramingSystem` (light steel or wood), the stud (column), track (beam) and header (beam) types, `spacing` 0.2 to 1.2 m, `double_top` (wood only; ignored for light steel) and `blocking`. `layout(&Project)` works in the wall's frame on its body centerline `u`: end studs flush with the wall ends (`END_STUD`), studs every `k·spacing` dropped where an opening's framing stands, king and jack studs, a header, a sill (windows) and cripples (`2k` above, `2k + 1` below) per opening, and blocking keyed by its left stud, as `FramingRole`/`FramingRoleKind` (pieces under `MIN_PIECE` skipped, `MAX_MEMBERS` bounds it); studs turn with the wall so their depth runs across it, and the beams have joins off. Its `inputs` are the wall and its doors and windows, and `member_level` keeps the members on the wall's level. Removing a wall removes its framing (`framings_of` in `remove_element`), copying a wall copies its framing (a framing alone is skipped), and `repair_wall_framings` keeps one per wall. `cc_engine::commands::wall_framing`: `wall_framing_create` (`framing_rows` through `type_or_row`/`catalog_type`; curved and already framed walls refused before any edit, one undo step, `{framings: [{id, wall, members}]}`), `element_get` `derived`, and `element_set` refusing a new `wall`. Plans draw only the members (nothing for the host) and IFC export leaves the host out. The app: the *Light Steel Frame* (Steel) and *Wood Frame* (Timber) buttons in the Structure tab's framing groups, `wall_framing_ui.rs` (the dialog, which frames the selected walls or the active level's; the palette rows with *Reset Members*; and the *Framing* row of a member) and `Icon::WallFraming`.
- **Connections** — `cc_core::connection`: `ElementKind::Connection(Connection { member, end: MemberEnd (Base, Start, End), kind: ConnectionKind (BasePlate, EndPlate, FinPlate, TimberBolts, Hanger), params: ConnectionParams })`, category `Connection`, a `TypeData::Connection {}` type created with the first one; lengths `LENGTH` 1 mm to 2 m, a plate's `width`/`length` 0 = from the section plus `edge`; rows and columns `COUNT` 1 to 10; refused by commands, reset by `repair_connections`, which keeps one connection per member end, the lowest id; removed with its member (`remove_element` → `Project::connections_of`), following its phases and option (`phase::phase_host`), never copied (`copy_set`), no placement of its own. `cc_geom::connection` (built after the beams into `ModelGeometry::connections`: `ConnectionGeom` with `Plate`s of oriented `Block`s and `Bolt`s of `Rod`s with washers, plate volume and mass at 7850 kg/m³, bolt count and length; `suits`: end plates and bolted joints at `BeamEnd::Column`, fin plates at columns or beams, hangers at beams; a `ConnectionWarning` and no parts otherwise; one steel solid per connection of category `Connection`, in the project's first metal); plans draw it where its member stands (`plan::draw_connections`). `cc_engine::commands::connection`: `connections_create` (`kind`, `members` or the level's, partial `params`; skips ends that have one; one undo step; `{created, skipped}`), `element_set` refuses a new member, end or kind, and `element_get` `derived` has `plate_volume`, `plate_mass`, `bolt_count`, `bolt_diameter`, `bolt_length` and `warning`; schedule fields `ConnectionKind`, `PlateMass`, `BoltCount`, `BoltDiameter`. IFC export writes an `IfcElementAssembly` `USERDEFINED` with the kind's code as `ObjectType`, aggregating an `IfcPlate` per plate and an `IfcMechanicalFastener` per bolt (`ANCHORBOLT` under base plates, else `BOLT`), typed per connection type, with an `IfcRelConnectsElements` from the member; import keeps the parts by the existing fallback. The app: *Structure → Connections* (steel Base Plate, End Plate, Fin Plate; timber Bolted Joint, Hanger), `UiCommand::Connection(kind)`, `connection_ui::place` (the selected members, else the active level's) and `connection_ui::props`, and the shared `Icon::Connection`.
- **Analytical model** — `cc_geom::analytical` (`analytical_model`, `AnalyticalModel`, `Node`, `Member`) and `cc_engine::commands::analytical` (`analytical_model`, `analytical_export`, the JSON document `format` `constructcad-analytical`, `FORMAT` 4). `Column::base_support` (`cc_core::BaseSupport` fixed/pinned, serde default fixed, `code`/`from_code`, `restraint()` as `[ux, uy, uz, rx, ry, rz]`; format 38) is set by `column_create`'s `base_support`, `element_set` and the *Base support* row of a structural column's properties; `Node::support` is the `Option<BaseSupport>` of the column bases on that node (fixed wins when two meet), and the document writes it as `support` (bool), `base_support` and `restraint`. `trim_column_tops` ends a column's top on the axis of the highest beam framed into it when that axis is within the beam's half depth (plus `REACH`) below the top and nothing else meets the top (no column above, no beam on it), so no free stub is left above the joint. The document also writes each section's profile `shape` and `material`, `materials` (`material_json`: `class`, `modulus` and `shear_modulus` (from an isotropic material's modulus and `poisson`) in Pa, `poisson`, `density`, `yield_strength`, `compressive_strength`, null where unknown), all `units` and the `axes` text (global axes, restraint order, section x across the width and y up the height, a column's x turned by its rotation). Tests: `crates/cc_engine/tests/analytical_supports.rs`, `analytical_arcs.rs`.
